Margins are D minus R across all votes. Baseline margins (one decimal) and total votes cast are 2024 certified results (MEDSL / National Archives, via PollCheck); Maine and Nebraska district margins are computed from certified district totals. 2028 EVs use the current apportionment (unchanged until the 2030 census). Group shares of the electorate are rough estimates. The county map is an estimate: each county gets its state's simulated result adjusted for an estimated urban/rural profile (derived from county land area), not actual county returns.
